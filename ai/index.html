AI rules

Last updated 2026-11-10

Minastic does not run an AI for you. If you use your own AI assistant with your workouts, you decide, and these are the rules.

The short version

  • No Minastic app runs an AI model or hosts an AI coach.
  • Your own AI can only reach your workout data through a connection you turn on.
  • Health data (heart rate, bodyweight, calories, check-in answers) is never part of an AI connection.
  • A plan you or your AI writes or changes is your responsibility.

What is available today

Nothing connects an AI to Minastic yet. The apps do not send your data to any AI service. The plan examples in the apps come from fixed rules and what you log, not from an AI model. You are free to copy your own workouts out of the app (export) and show them to any assistant you choose. Then your assistant's own terms apply, not ours.

The rules for a connection

When we add a way to connect your own AI assistant, these rules hold:

  • You turn it on. Nothing is connected until you say so, and you can turn it off again.
  • It is your AI. You pick the assistant. Minastic supplies none, and what an assistant writes is not advice from Minastic.
  • Workouts, plans and goals only. A connection carries your logged workouts, plans, ratings and goals. It does not carry health data: heart rate, bodyweight, calories and check-in answers. Minastic Lift keeps these on your phone only.
  • No hosted AI. We do not run an assistant on our servers.
  • Your responsibility. We cannot set limits on what your AI suggests. It can set any plan it likes, and the app applies the changes as given. Read the terms: any plan you or your own AI writes or changes is your responsibility.
  • Plans are examples. Any plan can lead to injury, especially without a proper warm-up and good technique. Pain or injury goes to a doctor or physiotherapist, not to an AI.

Not available yet. The connection itself is planned for Minastic Lift first, after its first release. This page describes the rules it will follow, not a feature you can use today.

Backup to your own Google Drive

We plan an optional backup of your data as plain files in a Minastic folder in your own Google Drive. If you turn it on, the app will tell you what is stored, that the files are not encrypted, and that your AI assistant can read them once you connect it through its own Google connection. The files go from your phone to your Drive. They do not pass through any server of ours, and we cannot see them. Not available yet.

Guides for your AI

We plan to publish research guides, a guide to writing programs for each app and a coach skill, openly and without an account, so your AI can read them. They will be listed on this site when they exist. Not published yet.

The short version

  • One person makes Minastic: Troels Rasmussen, Denmark. He is the data controller.
  • No ads, no tracking or analytics, no selling of data. We do not collect your CPR number.
  • Your data is on your phone and in our database in Frankfurt, Germany (EU).
  • Each app has its own section below that lists exactly what it collects and why.
  • You can see, export, correct and delete your data.

Health data: Minastic Lift keeps bodyweight, heart rate and calories on your phone only. Minastic Run, in its current testing version, works differently: its section says what it stores.

Who we are

Minastic is made by Troels Rasmussen, a private person in Denmark (no company yet). He is the data controller for the Minastic apps and for this website. Contact: deep.beep.studios.contact@gmail.com.

If Minastic moves to a company, we will tell you, and this notice will name it.

Legal basis

Each app's table names the legal basis for each kind of data. In short:

  • Contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)): what the app needs to work for you, such as your account and your workouts.
  • Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a); for health data Art. 9(2)(a)): only where an app asks for it. You can say no, and you can withdraw it later.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c), Art. 7(1)): the record of what you agreed to.

Who else receives data

  • Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage): our processor, with storage in the EU. One database project serves all Minastic apps, and each app keeps its data in its own tables.
  • Google: you can sign in with your Google account. Google handles the sign-in under its own privacy policy.
  • OpenFreeMap (Hungary, via Cloudflare), Minastic Run only: when the app shows a run map, it loads map tiles. This sends your IP address and the map area. No account data is sent.

No one else, unless the law requires it. None of the apps contains an ads or analytics SDK, and we do not sell data.

Where your data is stored

On your phone and watch, and in our database at Supabase in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). Supabase is a US company; support staff and some sub-processors may access data from outside the EU. This is covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

While your account exists. After you delete your account we delete your data within 30 days; encrypted backups roll off within a further 8 weeks. A short record that a deletion happened (no personal content) is kept for 3 years. Each app section adds what is specific to that app. The steps are on the delete page.

Your rights

You can see, export, correct and delete your data, withdraw consent, object and ask us to restrict processing. Write to deep.beep.studios.contact@gmail.com. We answer within one month. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing before the withdrawal. You can complain to Datatilsynet (datatilsynet.dk).

Withdrawing consent

Where an app asks for your consent, you can withdraw it in that app's Settings, with one tap. The app asks you to confirm first. What happens to your data then is in the app's section.

Age

Minastic is for people aged 16 and over.

Training guidance

Minastic gives training guidance. Use it at your own risk and seek professional advice when in doubt. It does not predict, prevent or diagnose injuries. See the terms.

This website

This is a static site on GitHub Pages (GitHub, a US company). It sets no cookies and has no analytics. When you open a page, GitHub receives your IP address and the request, as with any website, under GitHub's own privacy statement. The pages load the Archivo font from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address too. If you email us, we use your address only to answer you.

Changes

We will tell you in the app before we change this notice in a way that matters. The date at the top shows the last update.

Run: who else receives data

Supabase, Google and OpenFreeMap, as described above. OpenFreeMap is only used when Run shows a run map.

Run: how long we keep it

While your account exists. After you delete your account we delete your data within 30 days; encrypted backups roll off within a further 8 weeks. The consent log is kept for 3 years after deletion as proof. If you do not sign in for 24 months, we warn you and then delete the account.

Run: withdrawing consent

In Settings, one tap. If you withdraw the health-data consent, we delete your data from the cloud and backup stops. Your data stays on your phone. The app asks you to confirm first. If you turn off routes or model training, we delete the routes from the cloud or stop using your data for training.

In development, not on Google Play yet

Lift does not ask about, store or judge pain or injury. If something hurts, its help text only tells you to stop and see a doctor or physiotherapist, and nothing about it is stored. We do not sell data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking, and the app contains no third-party analytics or ads SDK.

What stays on your phone

Your bodyweight, heart rate and calories stay on your phone. They are not part of what syncs, and they never reach our servers. They leave the phone only if you export your data and tick that box.

The watch starts and logs a workout. It sends everything through your phone and never uploads to us by itself. History, analysis and your profile are on the phone only.

Lift: how long we keep it

While your account exists. In the app you can delete your Lift data, or your whole Minastic account (all apps). We delete it within 30 days, and encrypted backups roll off within a further 8 weeks. A short record that the deletion happened (the account number, what was deleted and when; no content) is kept for 3 years. After a whole-account deletion that number names nobody. Details are on the delete page.

Lift: withdrawing consent

Crash reports and diagnostics are off until you turn them on. You can turn them off again in Settings.

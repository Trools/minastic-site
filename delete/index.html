Delete your account

Last updated 2026-11-10

Delete in the app, or ask us by email. Both do the same thing.

In the app

Minastic Run

Open the app: You, then Settings, then Account and backup, then Delete account and data. Type DELETE to confirm. This deletes your account and your data in Minastic's cloud.

Minastic Lift

Lift is not released yet. In its first version, Settings, then Account, will offer two choices:

  • Delete Lift data: your Lift workouts, routines and settings. Your account and your other Minastic apps stay as they are.
  • Delete my whole Minastic account: every app's data, your sign-in and your terms acceptance.

The app offers to export your data first, and signs you out on every device afterwards. If you are offline, the deletion waits and is sent when you are online again.

Minastic Ride

Ride is not released yet.

Without the app, by email

Write to deep.beep.studios.contact@gmail.com from the email address of your Minastic account. Use the subject "Delete my Minastic account".

Say what you want deleted: the whole Minastic account, or only the Lift data (once Lift is out). We never ask for ID or a CPR number. If we cannot tell the request comes from your account, we will ask you to send it from the app instead.

We aim to reply within a week, and always within one month.

What gets deleted, and when

  • Deleted: your account, plans, workouts and runs, routines, check-ins, routes and settings, and your consents, in our database.
  • Within 30 days of your request. We aim to do it at once.
  • Backups roll off within a further 8 weeks. A deleted account is not put back from a backup.
  • Kept: a short record that the deletion happened, with no personal content, for 3 years. Run also keeps its consent log for 3 years, as its privacy notice says.
  • On your phone and watch: uninstalling an app removes its data there. Data on your phone is not ours to delete from afar.
  • Your own Google Drive: if you ever turn on the Drive backup (not available yet), those files are yours and stay until you delete them.
